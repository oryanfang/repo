xrhea 0.2.0 (pre-release)
Battery and charging tweak for jailbroken iOS 17

Battery and charging tweak for jailbroken iOS 17: detailed battery info, a real charge limit with scheduled full charge, heat protection, Low Power Mode automation, and short, readable alerts from the Dynamic Island.

Battery details

  • Maximum capacity, live power / voltage / current / temperature, time to full or to your limit
  • While charging, power is what the charger delivers (matches a USB power meter)
  • Charger info, capacity and cycle count
  • ⚠️ on readings that need attention, with what to do about it

Charge limit

  • Stop charging at 60–90%; the charger then powers the phone directly
  • When you plug in: keep the limit, or charge to 100% this time
  • Scheduled full charge: hold at the limit overnight, full by the time you set
  • Monthly reminder to charge fully once

Optimization

  • Heat protection while charging: stop charging or switch to intermittent charging at the set temperature (40 °C recommended)

Low Power Mode

  • Turn it on below a set level, off once charged to a set level, and on while locked
  • Keep 120 Hz in Low Power Mode on ProMotion models; other power savings stay on

Alerts

  • From the Dynamic Island (a long pill like the one iOS shows when you plug in, or a shorter one), or a banner on other phones
  • Charging: plugged in, target level, limit reached, fully charged, charging summary
  • Issues: slow charging, slowed by heat, loose cable, plugged in but not charging, hot, charging in the cold
  • Usage: standby drain, bedtime, high power draw
  • Each alert has its own switch and a Try It button; quiet during Focus with your own exceptions; tap to open battery details

Settings live in Settings › xrhea.

Pre-release. Only tested on iOS 17.3.1; some features may not work on other versions. Requires a roothide jailbreak. The settings are in English and Chinese (About › Language).

中文

iOS 17 越狱电池与充电插件：详细的电池信息、真正能停充的充电上限和定时充满、高温保护、低电量模式自动开关，还有从灵动岛弹出的、一眼看得懂的提醒。

电池信息

  • 最大容量，实时功率 / 电压 / 电流 / 温度，多久充满或充到上限
  • 充电时的功率是充电头送进手机的功率（和 USB 数显线对得上）
  • 充电器信息、容量和循环次数
  • 需要注意的读数标 ⚠️，并给出处理办法

充电上限

  • 充到 60%～90% 就停，之后电源直接给手机供电
  • 插电时可以选「按上限停」或「这次充满」
  • 定时充满：夜里先停在上限，在你设的时间前充满
  • 每月提醒充满一次

优化

  • 充电时的高温保护：达到设定温度（推荐 40℃）就停充或改为间歇充电

低电量模式

  • 低于设定电量自动开启、充到设定电量自动关闭、锁屏时开启
  • ProMotion 机型在低电量模式下保持 120Hz，其他省电措施不变

提醒

  • 从灵动岛弹出（和系统插电那条一样的长条，或者短条），没有灵动岛的机型用横幅
  • 充电：插电、到点、到上限停充、充满、拔线总结
  • 异常：慢充、过热降速、接触不良、插着却不充、过热、低温充电
  • 用电：锁屏掉电、睡前、高耗电
  • 每条单独开关，有「试试看」；专注模式时静默，可以自选照常提醒的几条；点提醒打开电池信息

设置在「设置 › xrhea」。

预览版。 只在 iOS 17.3.1 上测试过，其他版本可能部分功能不生效。需要 roothide 越狱环境。设置页有中文和英文（关于 › 语言）。
